    How these numbers are computed

    Character replies are counted as they are: mean length, longest reply. «Sounds cut off» looks at whether the reply ends on sentence-final punctuation. «Canned phrases» is the share of replies containing any phrase from a fixed list. «Same opening» compares the first eight characters of each reply. «Nearly identical neighbours» flags adjacent replies whose character bigrams overlap more than half. «Writing your lines» looks for blocks where your name is followed by a colon, or for placeholders like {{user}}. The rules were tuned on real before/after records I ran: the same log gives the same mean length, longest reply and «writing your lines» count here and in the full checker.

    Limits and privacy

    «Run the check» computes inside your browser: no upload, no network, no model calls; close the tab and nothing is left. Only if you press «Send» does the conversation and your reply address reach my server. This pass reads format and text only — it cannot follow the story, and it will not judge whether the character card is any good; that takes a human reading the card. It gives leads, not a verdict: whether a fix worked is settled by re-running the same log as a paired comparison.
